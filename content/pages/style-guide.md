These standards keep the registry accurate, readable and consistent.

## Accuracy

1. **Verify before you publish.** Only add information you know to be true.
2. **Cite your sources.** Every claim that someone might question needs a citation. Use `{{citation needed}}` to flag claims that still need one.
3. **Never invent details.** An empty section with a `{{todo}}` is better than a guess.

## Respect and privacy

- Write about Tom fairly and respectfully.
- Don't publish private information (home address, phone number, private documents) or anything Tom hasn't agreed to have on record.

## Writing style

- Write in a neutral, encyclopedic tone, in the third person.
- Open every article with a short summary paragraph that makes sense on its own.
- Use **bold** for the article's subject the first time it appears.
- Prefer short paragraphs and clear section headings.
- Dates: write *14 March 2024* in prose and *2024-03-14* in tables.

## Structure

- Link the first mention of any topic that has its own page: `[[Career]]`.
- Use `{{main|Page}}` at the top of a summary section that has its own full article.
- Put every dated event in the [[Timeline]] as well as in its article.
- Give every page at least one category.
